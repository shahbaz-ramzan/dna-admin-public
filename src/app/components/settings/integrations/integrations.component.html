Zapier Integrations

To use the Zapier integration, your account must possess DNA API keys and have been granted access to use Zapier. For detailed guidance on initiating this process and effectively utilizing the Zapier integration, learn more here .

Zapier Sync All

Clicking this button initiates a data retrieval process through Zapier, gathering all data from your account. The time required for this process depends on the volume of data in your account and can take up to several hours. Additionally, the amount of data you can synchronize and the frequency of syncs are determined by your Zapier plan. If the Sync All button appears disabled, it indicates that there is a pending sync in progress.
