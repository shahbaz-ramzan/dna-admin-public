Customize Reports

To customize the front page of your PDF reports, simply click on the edit button and type in your desired title in the designated field. The word "Report" will appear after the word you enter below. Keep in mind that the maximum character length is 50 characters, and no special characters are allowed. If no title is entered, the default name will appear on the cover page. For example: if you wish to have a report named "Acme Co Report", you would enter "Acme Co" below. The word "Report" will be automatically added to the end for you.
